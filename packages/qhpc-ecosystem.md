# QHPC-Ecosystem

QHPC-Ecosystem is the QSC integration layer for attributed capabilities,
controlled runners, persistent workflows, versioned APIs, and a browser
workbench. Its initial deployment allowlist includes QAppsWiki, ChatQEC,
OpenQEvo, FTQC, LightStim, and QIRIS/QFlow prototypes. (source:
https://github.com/QSCSoftwareEcosystem/QHPC-Ecosystem)
