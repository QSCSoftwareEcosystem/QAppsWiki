## Description

A non-CSS stabilizer code on which single-qubit Clifford gates together with qubit permutations realize the largest logical group attainable on two logical qubits.
It is the smallest distance-three stabilizer code with this property.

A stabilizer tableau for the code is  ([arXiv:2609.19250](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19250))
\begin{align}
\begin{array}{ccccccccc}
  X & Z & I & I & Y & X & I & I & I \\
  I & I & X & Z & I & I & Y & X & I \\
  Y & X & I & I & I & X & I & I & Z \\
  I & I & Y & X & I & I & I & X & Z \\
  Y & Y & I & I & Z & Y & I & I & I \\
  I & I & Y & Y & I & I & Z & Y & I \\
  X & X & X & X & Z & I & Z & I & X
\end{array}~.
\end{align}
The logical basis is $\overline{X}_1=IIZIYXIZI$, $\overline{X}_2=IYIIIYYXI$, $\overline{Z}_1=IXIXXIXII$, and $\overline{Z}_2=IYIYIYIYI$  ([arXiv:2609.19250](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19250)).

(source: raw/error-correction-zoo.md)

## Transversal gates

- Single-qubit Clifford gates together with qubit permutations realize the logical group $O^{+}(4,2)$ of order 72  ([arXiv:2609.19250](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19250)).
In the displayed logical basis, this group is generated by $H_1$, $H_2$, and $\mathrm{CZ}_{12}$.
A different logical basis gives the generators $H_1$, $S_1$, and $\mathrm{SWAP}_{12}$  ([arXiv:2609.19250](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19250)).
This is the largest logical group attainable this way at $k=2$, exceeding the maximum of 48 attainable by a CSS code  ([arXiv:2609.19250](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19250)).
