## Description

A $⟦54,6,5⟧$ qubit stabilizer code whose encoder-respecting form is the graph of a five-cover of the icosahedron  ([arXiv:2411.14448](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14448)).
The covering-space construction avoids the weight-three logical operators that occur for the bare icosahedral graph  ([arXiv:2411.14448](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.14448)).
