## Description

Group-based code whose codewords realize 3D topological order defined by a finite group $G$.

Excitations are characterized by irreducible representations of a generalization of the quantum double (algebra), often called the quantum triple  ([arXiv:1404.4618](https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.4618), [arXiv:1709.04924](https://arxiv.org/abs/1709.04924)).
