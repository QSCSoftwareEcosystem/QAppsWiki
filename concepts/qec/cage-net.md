## Description

A commuting-projector code family obtained by coupling layers of two-dimensional topological orders and condensing extended one-dimensional flux strings  ([arXiv:1806.04687](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.04687)).
A modern lattice realization starts from isotropic stacks of $G$-graded string-net models  ([arXiv:2505.13604](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13604)).
The family includes stabilizer examples such as the $\mathbb{Z}_2$ string-membrane-net realization of the X-cube model  ([arXiv:1812.01613](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.01613)) as well as non-stabilizer examples with non-Abelian restricted-mobility excitations  ([arXiv:1806.04687](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.04687)).
String-membrane-net and cage-net constructions can realize the same fracton phases; for isotropic stacks, this equivalence can be understood via generalized local unitaries  ([arXiv:2505.13604](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13604)).
The cage-net construction can be used to realize various fracton phases, stabilizer and otherwise.
