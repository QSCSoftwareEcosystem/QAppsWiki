## Description

Smallest member of a family of CSS phantom HGP codes obtained from the hypergraph product of a classical simplex code and a repetition code.

A stabilizer tableau for an equivalent code is given by 
\begin{align}
\begin{array}{ccccccc}
  Z & Z & I & I & I & I & Z \\
  I & I & Z & I & Z & I & Z \\
  I & I & I & Z & I & Z & Z \\
  I & X & X & X & I & I & X \\
  X & I & I & I & X & X & X
\end{array}~.
\end{align}
