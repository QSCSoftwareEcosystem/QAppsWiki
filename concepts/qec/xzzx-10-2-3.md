## Description

Rotated toric code that is the CSS form of the twisted XZZX toric code with parameters $a=1$, $b=3$  ([arXiv:1108.5490](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.5490), [arXiv:2101.09349](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.09349)),
related to the XZZX form by Hadamard on the $B$-sublattice.
It is also the symplectic double (a.k.a. genus-one double cover) of the $⟦5,1,3⟧$ five-qubit perfect code  ([arXiv:1212.6703](https://arxiv.org/abs/1212.6703), [arXiv:2406.09951](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09951)), the symplectic double of the $⟦5,1,2⟧$ rotated surface code  ([arXiv:2406.09951](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.09951)), and a BCC code  ([arXiv:2509.06865](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06865)).

A stabilizer tableau for this code is
\begin{align}
\begin{array}{cccccccccc}
  Z & Z & I & Z & Z & I & I & I & I & I \\
  I & I & Z & Z & I & Z & Z & I & I & I \\
  I & I & I & I & Z & Z & I & Z & Z & I \\
  Z & I & I & I & I & I & Z & Z & I & Z \\
  X & I & X & X & I & I & I & I & I & X \\
  I & X & X & I & X & X & I & I & I & I \\
  I & I & I & X & X & I & X & X & I & I \\
  I & I & I & I & I & X & X & I & X & X
\end{array}~.
\end{align}

## Protection

Distance 3, correcting any single-qubit error.
Achieves the lower bound $n \geq d^2+1 = 10$ for weight-four GB codes of odd distance  ([arXiv:2203.17216](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.17216)).

## Transversal gates

- Transversal Hadamard-SWAP: qubit permutation $m \mapsto -m$ (mod 10) paired with sublattice swap $A \leftrightarrow B$, followed by Hadamard on all qubits  ([arXiv:2509.06865](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06865)).
- Logical Hadamard without SWAP via $m \mapsto 3m$ (mod 10) followed by Hadamard  ([arXiv:2509.06865](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06865)).
