## Description

Member of a family of non-Abelian 2D topological codes, defined by a finite group $ G $, that serves as a generalization of the 2D color code (for which $G=\mathbb{Z}_2 \times \mathbb{Z}_2$).
Hamiltonian terms are built from group-based right- and left-multiplication $X$-type operators together with $Z$-type operators.
