## Description

Member of a family of pure odd-length distance-two CWS codes with parameters $((2m+1,3 \times 2^{2m-3},2))$ for all $m \geq 2$, constructed recursively from the $((5,6,2))$ code  ([arXiv:quant-ph/9704043](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9704043)) ([arXiv:cs/0610159](https://arxiv.org/abs/cs/0610159)) ([doi:10.1090/S0002-9947-07-04242-0](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-07-04242-0), [arXiv:1907.04537](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.04537)).
