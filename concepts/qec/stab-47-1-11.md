## Description

A $⟦47,1,11⟧$ self-dual CSS code constructed from a binary quadratic-residue code.
It is one of the shortest known qubit stabilizer codes of distance eleven that realizes the full Clifford group transversally  ([arXiv:2408.12752](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12752)).

(source: raw/error-correction-zoo.md)

## Protection

Detects up to 10-qubit errors and corrects up to 5-qubit errors.

## Decoders

- Algebraic decoder  ([arXiv:0711.1556](https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.1556)).

## Transversal gates

- All logical Clifford gates are realized transversally  ([arXiv:2408.12752](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.12752)).
