## Description

Eleven-qubit pure stabilizer code that is the smallest qubit stabilizer code to correct two-qubit errors.
It can be obtained from the non-additive dodecacode by puncturing  ([arXiv:quant-ph/9608006](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9608006)).

A stabilizer tableau for the code is given by  ([arXiv:quant-ph/9705052](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9705052)) ([arXiv:quant-ph/0406063](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0406063))
\begin{align}
\begin{array}{ccccccccccc}
  Z & Z & Z & Z & Z & Z & I & I & I & I & I \\
  X & X & X & X & X & X & I & I & I & I & I \\
  I & I & I & Z & X & Y & Y & Y & Y & X & Z \\
  I & I & I & X & Y & Z & Z & Z & Z & Y & X \\
  Z & Y & X & I & I & I & Z & Y & X & I & I \\
  X & Z & Y & I & I & I & X & Z & Y & I & I \\
  I & I & I & Z & Y & X & X & Y & Z & I & I \\
  I & I & I & X & Z & Y & Z & X & Y & I & I \\
  Z & X & Y & I & I & I & Z & Z & Z & X & Y \\
  Y & Z & X & I & I & I & Y & Y & Y & Z & X
\end{array}~.
\end{align}
The code has paired support despite not being Hermitian  ([arXiv:2605.15344](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15344)).

(source: raw/error-correction-zoo.md)

## Protection

Smallest stabilizer code that protects against errors on any two qubits. Detects four-qubit errors.

## Encoders

- Encoding circuit consisting of 32 gates constructed from reinforcement learning  ([arXiv:2311.04750](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.04750)).
