## Description

Encodes a finite-dimensional *logical* Hilbert space into a *physical* Hilbert space associated with a category.
The categories of interest are typically fusion categories, which subsume all finite groups and many state spaces associated with topological codes. 
Codes on modular fusion categories are often associated with a particular topological quantum field theory (TQFT), as the data of such theories is described by such categories.
