## Description

A block quantum code on $n$ subsystems that either detects or corrects errors on at most two subsystems, i.e., have distance $\leq 5$.

See Refs.  ([arXiv:quant-ph/9704043](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9704043), [arXiv:0801.0831](https://arxiv.org/abs/0801.0831), [arXiv:0901.1353](https://arxiv.org/abs/0901.1353)) for small-distance codes.
