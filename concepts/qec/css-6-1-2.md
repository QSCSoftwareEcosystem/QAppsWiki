## Description

A six-qubit CSS stabilizer code that is an example of a semi-self-dual CSS code, i.e., a CSS code whose $X$-type stabilizers are contained in the $Z$-type stabilizers  ([arXiv:2507.10519](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10519)).

A stabilizer tableau for the code is given by 
\begin{align}
\begin{array}{cccccc}
  Z & I & Z & I & I & Z \\
  I & Z & Z & I & Z & I \\
  I & I & I & Z & Z & Z \\
  X & I & X & X & X & I \\
  I & X & X & X & I & X
\end{array}~.
\end{align}
An equivalent self-dual semi-CSS presentation has generators $XXXXII$, $IIXXXX$, $ZZZZII$, $IIZZZZ$, and $IYIYIY$  ([arXiv:2507.10519](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10519)).
A qubit permutation $0\mapsto 0$, $1\mapsto 2$, $2\mapsto 4$, $3\mapsto 5$, $4\mapsto 1$, $5\mapsto 3$, followed by the same one-qubit Clifford on every qubit sending $X\mapsto Z$, $Z\mapsto Y$, and $Y\mapsto X$, converts the CSS generators to that semi-CSS presentation.
This makes the code a concrete example of the equivalence between the semi-self-dual CSS and self-dual semi-CSS cases in the $U(\ell,R_8)$ family  ([arXiv:2507.10519](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10519)).

## Protection

Detects any single-qubit error as a distance-two stabilizer code.

## Transversal gates

- The physical gate $S^{\dagger}_{0}S^{\dagger}_{1}S^{\dagger}_{2}S_{3}S_{4}S_{5}$ implements the logical action $\bar{S}$ .
- In the equivalent self-dual semi-CSS presentation obtained by fixing one logical qubit of the $⟦6,2,2⟧$ $C_6$ code to $|Y^{-}\rangle_L$, transversal gates support a fault-tolerant magic-state-preparation circuit  ([arXiv:2507.10519](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10519)).
