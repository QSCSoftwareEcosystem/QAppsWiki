## Description

A qubit stabilizer code that is constructed from a normal rational curve and that is relevant to testing the purity of an entangled Bell state shared by two parties  ([arXiv:quant-ph/0205128](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0205128)).
Purity-testing stabilizer codes have been generalized to come from more general non-projective codes  ([arXiv:2503.14003](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14003)).
