## Description

Member of one of several families of lifted-product codes that consist of sparsely interconnected stacks of surface codes.

(source: raw/error-correction-zoo.md)

## Protection

Code distance is linear with $n$ up to some maximum number of qubits.

## Threshold

- $2.9\%$ and $3.2\%$ under phenomenological noise and BP+OSD decoding for two families of LCS codes.

## Code capacity threshold

- $6.7\%$ and $7.7\%$ under bit-flip noise and BP+OSD decoding for two families of LCS codes.
