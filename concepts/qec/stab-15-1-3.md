## Description

A $⟦15,1,3⟧$ quantum Reed-Muller code that is most easily thought of as a tetrahedral 3D color code.
It can be constructed as a CSS code from the $[15,5,8]$ punctured Reed-Muller code and its even subcode, which explains its transversal $T^\dagger$ gate .

This code contains 15 qubits, represented by four vertices, four face centers, six edge centers, and one body center.
The tetrahedron is cellulated into four identical polyhedron cells by connecting the body center to all four face centers, where each face center is then connected by three adjacent edge centers.
Each colored cell corresponds to a weight-eight $X$-check, and each face corresponds to a weight-4 $Z$-check.
A logical $Z$ is any weight-3 $Z$-string along an edge of the entire tetrahedron.
The logical $X$ is any weight-7 $X$-face of the entire tetrahedron.

The code can also be thought of as a code on all corners of a tesseract except one  ([doi:10.1098/rsta.2011.0494](https://doi.org/10.1098/rsta.2011.0494)).

In the qubit order given by the nonzero binary four-tuples $0001,0010,\ldots,1111$, one stabilizer tableau for the code is

  Z & Z & I & I & Z & Z & I & I & I & I & I & I & I & I & I \\
  Z & Z & I & I & I & I & I & Z & I & I & Z & I & I & I & I \\
  Z & Z & I & I & I & I & I & I & Z & Z & I & I & I & I & I \\
  Z & Z & I & I & I & I & I & I & I & I & I & Z & I & I & Z \\
  Z & Z & I & I & I & I & I & I & I & I & I & I & Z & Z & I \\
  Z & I & Z & Z & I & Z & I & I & I & I & I & I & I & I & I \\
  Z & I & Z & I & I & I & I & Z & I & Z & I & I & I & I & I \\
  Z & I & Z & I & I & I & I & I & I & I & I & Z & I & Z & I \\
  Z & I & I & Z & I & I & I & Z & I & I & I & I & Z & I & I \\
  I & I & I & I & I & I & I & X & X & X & X & X & X & X & X \\

The logical $\ket{\overline{0}}$ and $\ket{\overline{1}}$ states are superpositions of computational-basis words of weight $0 \bmod 8$ and $7 \bmod 8$.

## Encoders

- Fault-tolerant logical zero and logical plus state preparation using reinforcement learning  ([arXiv:2402.17761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17761)).

## Magic scaling exponent

Magic-state yield parameter $ \gamma= \log_d (n/k)\approx 2.47$  ([arXiv:1612.07330](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.07330)) ([arXiv:1703.07847](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.07847)).

## Transversal gates

- A transversal logical $T$ is implemented by applying a $T^\dagger$ gate on every qubit  ([arXiv:quant-ph/9610011](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9610011), [arXiv:1403.2734](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.2734), [arXiv:1612.07330](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.07330)). This is the smallest qubit stabilizer code with a (strongly) transversal gate outside of the Clifford group  ([arXiv:2210.14066](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.14066)).
- Transversal logical $CS$ gates between code blocks, together with transversal $T$ and CNOT, can be used to realize IQP-like sampling architectures on a hypercube connectivity graph  ([arXiv:2404.19005](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.19005)).

## General gates

- Code is often used in magic-state distillation protocols because of its transversal $T$ gate  ([arXiv:quant-ph/0403025](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/0403025)). In the original 15-to-1 protocol, 15 noisy $R_{\pi/8}\ket{+}$ magic states are used to implement the transversal $T$ by compressed gate teleportation, the resulting encoded state is post-selected by 15-qubit error detection, and decoding yields an output magic state with error probability $O(p^3)$ when the input states have independent error probability $p$ .
- Non-transversal logical Hadamard  ([arXiv:2510.08402](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08402)).

## Decoders

- Decoding has been studied for the BP, BP+OSD, and AutDEC decoders  ([arXiv:2503.01738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.01738)).
- An exact ML decoder based on the Walsh-Hadamard transform was developed for error correction and gauge fixing in the alternating Steane/$⟦15,1,3⟧$ Clifford + $T$ protocol  ([arXiv:1509.03239](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.03239)).

## Fault tolerance

- A fault-tolerant universal gate set can be done via code switching between the Steane code and the $⟦15,1,3⟧$ code  ([arXiv:1509.03239](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.03239), [arXiv:1304.3709](https://arxiv.org/abs/1304.3709), [arXiv:1403.2734](https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.2734), [arXiv:1703.03860](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.03860), [arXiv:2210.14074](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.14074)).
- Fault-tolerant logical zero and logical plus state preparation using reinforcement learning  ([arXiv:2402.17761](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17761)).

## Realizations

- Trapped ions: code switching between the Steane code and the $⟦15,1,3⟧$ code as well as magic-state preparation and logical Bell measurements on the Steane code realized on the 28-qubit H2-1 device by Quantinuum  ([arXiv:2506.14169](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.14169)).
- MBQC with the $⟦15,1,3⟧$ code has been demonstrated in neutral atom arrays by the Lukin group  ([arXiv:2506.20661](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20661)).
