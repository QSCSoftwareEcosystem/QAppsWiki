## Description

A member of a family of Galois-qudit CSS codes constructed from skew-cyclic classical codes over rings  ([doi:10.1016/j.disc.2020.112189](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2020.112189)).
See related study  ([doi:10.1007/s12095-018-0299-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12095-018-0299-0)) that uses cyclic codes over rings.
