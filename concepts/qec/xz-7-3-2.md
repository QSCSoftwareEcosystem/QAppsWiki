## Description

A seven-qubit pure CSS code that corrects a single $X$ or a single $Z$ error, but not a single $Y$ error.
It is the punctured version of the $⟦8,3,2⟧$ hypercube quantum code and is a phantom code  ([arXiv:2601.20927](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20927)).

A CSS stabilizer tableau for the code is given by  ([arXiv:quant-ph/9705052](https://arxiv.org/abs/quant-ph/9705052))
\begin{align}
\begin{array}{ccccccc}
  Z & Z & Z & I & I & I & Z \\
  I & Z & I & Z & I & Z & Z \\
  Z & Z & I & I & Z & Z & I \\
  X & X & X & X & X & X & X
\end{array}~.
\end{align}

(source: raw/error-correction-zoo.md)

## Protection

Distance two. As the $k=3$ punctured hypercube code, it saturates the general qubit phantom-code bound $n\geq 2^k-1$  ([arXiv:2604.15111](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.15111)).
