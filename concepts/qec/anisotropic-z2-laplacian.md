## Description

A graph-based analogue of a Type-I fracton phase with lineon-like restricted mobility  ([arXiv:2210.03727](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03727), [arXiv:2209.07987](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.07987)).

On generic sparse graphs, the Laplacian seed is not rank deficient enough to generate fracton order  ([arXiv:2312.08462](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.08462)).
Instead, the model exhibits partially confined point-like excitations in a phase more akin to nonlocal topological order.
