## Description

CSS code obtained by binarizing a $⟦5,1,3⟧_4$ Galois-qudit CSS code in the self-dual normal basis $\{\omega,\omega^2\}$.

A stabilizer tableau for the binarized code is  ([arXiv:2601.20927](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20927))
\begin{align}
\begin{array}{cccccccccc}
  Z & I & Z & I & Z & I & Z & I & I & I \\
  I & Z & I & Z & I & Z & I & Z & I & I \\
  I & I & Z & I & Z & Z & I & Z & Z & I \\
  I & I & I & Z & Z & I & Z & Z & I & Z \\
  X & I & X & I & X & I & X & I & I & I \\
  I & X & I & X & I & X & I & X & I & I \\
  I & I & X & I & I & X & X & X & X & I \\
  I & I & I & X & X & X & X & I & I & X
\end{array}~.
\end{align}

(source: raw/error-correction-zoo.md)

## Transversal gates

- The code is permutation-equivalent to its Hadamard dual: transversal physical Hadamards followed by swapping the two qubits in each binarized $\mathbb{F}_4$ coordinate preserve the codespace and implement a logical $H^{\otimes 2}$ up to logical $\mathrm{SWAP}$. This is the binarized version of the permutation-assisted Hadamard available from plain self-duality of the starting $q=4$ Galois-qudit CSS code  ([arXiv:2601.20927](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20927)).
