## Description

Subsystem version of the rotated surface code.

For example, a $⟦3d^2-2d,1,d⟧$ family admits weight-six $X,Z$-type stabilizer generators on hexagonal plaquettes and weight-three gauge generators on bow-tie shaped regions on the dual lattice.
