## Description

Variant of the Guth-Lubotzky hyperbolic surface code that uses regular tessellations of 4-dimensional hyperbolic space.

(source: raw/error-correction-zoo.md)

## Protection

Nonvanishing rate and asymptotic distance lower bounded by $n^0.1$.

## Rate

Nonvanishing rate and asymptotic distance lower bounded by $n^0.1$. However, the smallest number of physical qubits in this family is 234,000.
